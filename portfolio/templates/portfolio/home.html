How I work

Research before building

A product without findings is a product waiting to fail. I start by understanding the data, the constraints, and the failure cases, then choose the tool that fits the job.

Maintainability over cleverness

Most of what I ship runs on Django and DRF because they are data-heavy, secure, and well supported. PostgreSQL is my default database. Flutter covers mobile and desktop from one codebase. Go is next, for its speed and concurrency model.
